A village with a monthly pulse.

The working model: artist residencies and daily wellness feed one public-facing community weekend each month. Studios open, food is shared, installations light up, and a slow art-car procession moves through approved internal routes.

  1. Weeks 1 to 3Residence

    Artists make, guests stay, and wellness facilities operate at a calm everyday cadence.

  2. Final weekendAssembly

    Open studios, community supper, workshops, performance, installations, and a managed art-car procession.

  3. Week 4Afterglow

    Document the work, measure the weekend, reset the grounds, and invite the next cohort.

Proposed programming only. Event frequency, routes, capacity, amplified sound, food service, fire effects, vehicles, and public access require operational, insurance, safety, neighbor, and permitting review.

The vision earns trust in steps.

  1. Know the property

    Reconcile ownership, operations, occupancy, utilities, and every serviceable site.

  2. Stabilize the base

    Protect resident income and repair the systems that make every future stay dependable.

  3. Open the pilot

    Concentrate the first 20-30 sites into one legible, measurable Colony district.

  4. Prove the rhythm

    Show occupancy, repeat stays, hosted programs, operating discipline, and visible progress.

The Founding Circle

A place to feel human again.

The Colony starts with a simple belief: time outside, shared creative work, and real community can help people feel more connected. We are gathering the first people who want to help bring that place to life.

Not an audience.
A circle of makers.
