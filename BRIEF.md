# The Colony website brief

**Page job:** make The Colony feel like a real place with an achievable first transformation, while giving guests, hosts, builders, collaborators, and future investors a reason to follow the project.

## Audience

- Friends, family, and collaborators seeing the property for the first time
- Retreat hosts, artists, operators, and culture builders
- A possible anchor investor aligned with Burning Man and participatory culture
- Future qualified investors who receive separate, private diligence materials

## Story

1. **Place:** open on real land and the Jacumba mountains.
2. **Proof:** show the grounds and historical property baseline.
3. **Transformation:** compare current conditions with clearly labeled concept visuals.
4. **Experience:** move through a plausible morning, afternoon, and night.
5. **Plan:** show a contained 20 to 30 site pilot and the proof needed before expansion.
6. **Participation:** route visitors toward staying, hosting, building, or following.

## Visual system

Cinematic desert documentary. Black, sand, mineral green, and ember. Large editorial typography, real property texture, restrained chrome, and motion that reveals geography and change. The existing Scrollcraft engine remains the interaction layer.

## Trust rules

- Label current photographs and illustrative concepts at the point of use.
- Treat the 2022 appraisal figures as historical baselines that need current verification.
- Do not imply that the project controls the property or can offer property-backed securities until agreements and counsel confirm that.
- Keep public hospitality storytelling separate from any private fundraising materials.
- No public investment terms, projected returns, urgency, or investor intake until counsel selects and clears the offering pathway.

## Current prototype scope

The first pass implements the full narrative spine with four current-property photographs and one concept render. Next passes should add a verified property map, matched-camera Today / Pilot / Vision pairs, short drone sequences, operators and people, and real pilot milestones.
